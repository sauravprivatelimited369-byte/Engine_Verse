1 · The rule: Ohm's law

Current is proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance.

I = V / R  —  current (A) = voltage (V) ÷ resistance (Ω)

P = V × I  —  power (W) = voltage (V) × current (A)

Worked example. 12 V across 6 Ω gives 12 / 6 = 2 A. Swap in a 12 Ω resistor and the same supply gives 12 / 12 = 1 A: double the resistance, halve the current.
