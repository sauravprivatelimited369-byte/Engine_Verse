Your data

We store your email, a scrypt hash of your password, your profile and your learning history. Nothing is sold or shared. Sessions store only a hash of the cookie token.

Security

  • Passwords hashed with scrypt (N=16384)
  • HttpOnly, SameSite cookies
  • CSRF token on every state change
  • Rate-limited login with lockout
  • Optional authenticator (TOTP) and one-time recovery codes
  • Email confirmation when a mail transport is configured
  • No third-party scripts or trackers
