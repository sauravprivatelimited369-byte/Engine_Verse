Browser Web App & what works offline

In desktop Chrome, Edge or Brave, click the install icon in the address bar; in Safari on Mac, use File → Add to Dock…; on iPhone and iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Offline honesty: Native iOS, macOS and Android apps bundle all 93 subjects and 406 topic summaries, the study planner, streaks and your learning record offline on-device with no account or analytics. Full notes, daily practice and WebAssembly compilers load over HTTPS. In the browser Web App, pages you have already visited are cached for offline reading while unvisited pages and server-judged submissions need a connection.
