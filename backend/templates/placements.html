What actually gets people hired

  1. Fundamentals you can explain, not recite. Interviewers probe one layer deeper than the definition. Our notes include the "why" and the interview questions for exactly this reason.
  2. Two or three projects you understand deeply. One project you can defend beats five you copied. Every project brief here lists the questions you will be asked about it.
  3. Consistent practice. A 30-day streak beats a weekend marathon. The daily practice set and spaced repetition exist to make consistency easy.
  4. Clear communication. Say what you are doing while you code. Silence reads as confusion.
