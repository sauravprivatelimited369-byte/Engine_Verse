Privacy

What we store

Your email address, a scrypt hash of your password, your profile details, and your learning history: topics read, questions answered, code you submitted, flashcard reviews and XP.

What we never do

  • We do not use third-party authentication providers. Sign-in happens entirely on this server.
  • We do not load any third-party script, font, analytics pixel or advertising tag.
  • We do not sell your data or publish plaintext learner records. An operator may store encrypted backups with a third party as described below.
  • We do not store your password in any recoverable form — only a salted scrypt hash.
  • Session cookies store only a SHA-256 digest of the token, so a database leak cannot be replayed as a login.

Code you submit

Submitted code is stored against your account. A production instance runs it only when its operator configures an external judge; that provider's isolation and data handling are the operator's responsibility. With no judge configured, code execution is unavailable.

Where data lives

On a live instance your account and progress are stored in the operator's private database, not in the public source-code repository or in GitHub Pages. An operator may also retain encrypted off-site database backups, including in a separate private GitHub repository. Ask the operator about backup retention and deletion before registering. A static reading copy, if offered separately, keeps its browser streak only on that device and does not sync it into an account.

Your control

You can change your password, sign out any session, adjust what your public portfolio shows, and delete your account from Settings. Deletion removes your live account rows; older backups can still contain them until the operator expires or removes those backups.
