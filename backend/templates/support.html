Report a bug or a wrong answer

Open an issue on GitHub. A good report includes:

  • The page URL, and what you expected compared with what you saw.
  • Your browser, or app version, and your device.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, plus a screenshot if it helps.

Never paste passwords, one-time codes, phone numbers or other personal data into a public issue.

Suggest content

Missing a subject, topic, practice problem, open-access book or recurring opportunity? Open an issue that describes it and links to a reliable source. Content is reviewed before it is published. We do not link to pirated books or videos.

Report a security issue privately

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Report it privately through GitHub's security advisory form instead, so it can be fixed before details are public. The full policy is in SECURITY.md (opens in a new tab).
