The study loop

Learn → practise → review

The same three moves for every subject, so you always know what to do next.

  1. 1

    Learn it properly

    A definition, the intuition, a diagram where it helps, and a worked example. Four depths per topic, from a quick refresher to the full derivation.

    Browse notes
  2. 2

    Practise under pressure

    Daily sets, question banks and judged coding problems. A wrong answer is filed in your mistake notebook instead of disappearing.

    Open practice
  3. 3

    Review until it holds

    Flashcards come back on a spaced schedule, and your progress page shows what is actually sticking rather than what you merely opened.

    Review cards
