# Code of Conduct

Forge is built for students, many of them learning in public for the
first time. We want every contributor and learner to feel safe asking a basic
question or pointing out a mistake.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in a
  second language.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain why, and suggest a fix.
- Accept correction gracefully, and credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone's personal details, and never paste real
  learner data into an issue or pull request.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background, caste, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Plagiarism presented as original work, or spam and self-promotion.
- Repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer privately. Use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/sauravprivatelimited369-byte/Engine_Verse/security/advisories/new)
form when it is enabled, or open an issue that only asks for a private contact
and contains no details. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn someone, or block them
temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious and repeated the
behaviour is.
